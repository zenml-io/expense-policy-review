# Lumenfield Analytics expense policy

**Synthetic.** This document is fictional. It is not a real company's policy.

Company: Lumenfield Analytics. Currency: USD. The figures below are demo data.

## POL-MEALS-01 — Meals and per-diem

Employees may claim a meal while working away from their home office, up to 75.00 USD per person per day. A claim of exactly 75.00 USD is within the cap. The cap applies to the total for that person on that calendar day. A per-diem of 75.00 USD may be claimed instead of itemized meals for a day with an overnight stay. Do not claim a per-diem and an itemized meal for the same day. Splitting one meal, one day, or one bill into more than one submission so each part sits under 75.00 USD is not allowed. If the memo says the charge was split, or points at another submission that is the other part of the same meal, flag the expense for a person to review.

## POL-CLIENT-01 — Client entertainment

A meal or other entertainment with a client is allowed up to 150.00 USD per attendee, including the employee, when the memo names the client company and every attendee. A figure of exactly 150.00 USD per attendee is within the cap. If the memo does not name the client company or the attendees, flag the expense for a person to review. Do not guess who attended. This section does not allow alcohol. Alcohol follows the alcohol section.

## POL-ALCOHOL-01 — Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable. That includes beer or wine the employee drinks alone, including before a flight. Exception: beer or wine served at a client dinner may be claimed up to 40.00 USD per person, and only when Finance has already approved that dinner in writing. A memo that says a manager approved it is not written Finance approval. If the employee claims the exception and the written approval is not in the expense record, flag the expense for a person to review. Do not approve the exception from the memo alone, and do not deny it only because the memo is incomplete.

## POL-AIRFARE-01 — Travel and airfare

Airfare is reimbursable in an economy cabin only. The domestic round-trip cap is 800.00 USD. The international round-trip cap is 1800.00 USD. A fare exactly at the cap that applies is within the limit. Buy the ticket at least 14 days before departure. If the memo does not say whether the trip is domestic or international, or does not say how far ahead it was booked, flag the expense for a person to review.

## POL-LODGING-01 — Lodging

Lodging is reimbursable up to 250.00 USD per night in a standard city and 350.00 USD per night in Port Selene. A night priced exactly at the cap for that city is within the limit. The cap is per night, not per stay. Port Selene is the only high-cost city in this synthetic policy. Every other city is standard. If the memo does not give the number of nights or the city, flag the expense for a person to review instead of treating the whole stay as one night.

## POL-GROUND-01 — Ground transport and mileage

A rideshare or taxi trip is reimbursable up to 75.00 USD. A trip to or from an airport is reimbursable up to 150.00 USD. A trip priced exactly at the cap that applies is within the limit. Personal-car mileage is reimbursable at 0.70 USD per mile, up to 200.00 USD per trip. The memo must state the miles. If the miles are missing, flag the expense for a person to review.

## POL-SOFTWARE-01 — Software and subscriptions

An individual software subscription is reimbursable up to 50.00 USD per month. A subscription of exactly 50.00 USD per month is within the cap. Divide an annual charge by 12 and compare the result with 50.00 USD. If the memo does not say whether the charge is monthly or annual, flag the expense for a person to review. A team license, or any subscription above the cap, needs a manager's written approval. If that approval is not in the expense record, flag the expense for a person to review.

## POL-GIFTS-01 — Gifts

A gift for a client is reimbursable up to 50.00 USD per recipient per calendar year. A gift of exactly 50.00 USD is within the cap. Cash and gift cards are not reimbursable. A gift for an employee is not reimbursable. If the memo does not name the recipient, flag the expense for a person to review.

## POL-HOME-01 — Home office

A one-time home-office setup, such as a desk, chair, or lamp, is reimbursable up to 300.00 USD when a receipt is attached. Furniture priced above 150.00 USD also needs written Facilities approval before purchase. If the employee says that approval exists but it is not in the expense record, flag the expense for a person to review. Home internet is reimbursable up to 50.00 USD per month. Rent and decorations are not reimbursable.

## POL-RECEIPTS-01 — Receipts

A receipt is required when the amount is above 25.00 USD. An amount of exactly 25.00 USD does not require a receipt. A missing receipt above 25.00 USD is missing required information: flag the expense for a person to review. Do not deny an otherwise plausible expense only because the receipt is missing, and do not approve it by assuming a receipt exists.
